How to make a price list for your services (with 4 examples)

What to put on each line of a price list, how to choose the unit, and 4 example lists from different kinds of work.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

7 min read · Last updated 23 October 2026

In short
  • Each line needs a name, a unit, a price in RM and a note on what is included.
  • Use 1 unit per line, such as per hour, per sq ft, per package or per month.
  • When the price varies, give each version its own line, such as half day and full day. Use a "from" price only when you can't tell until the job, like an aircond gas top-up.
  • Give small costs their own lines: transport, disposal, materials, rush jobs and a minimum charge.

A price list is your own list of what you charge: each service, its unit and its price. You set it up once and price every quotation from it, so the same job gets the same price every time.

It also helps when a customer asks "how much?" before booking. You can answer from the list instead of working the price out again.

Turning the list into a quotation is covered in How to write a quotation.

What to put on each line

  • Name: the words your customer uses, plus whatever changes the price ("Website, up to 5 pages").
  • Unit: what the price is for, such as 1 hour, 1 sq ft, 1 package or 1 month.
  • Price: in RM, with 2 decimals (RM 150.00).
  • Note: what the price includes, and what it does not ("2 rounds of changes included. You supply the text and photos.").

Put the lines under a few headings, such as Packages and Add-ons, so you can find an item quickly.

In Invois, you can type the list, say it, or photograph the one you already have, and AI reads the items and prices; you choose which to add. More in Your price list in Invois.

How to choose the unit

  • Per unit or per person for things you can count: aircond units, logo designs, haircuts, catering per pax.
  • Per sq ft or per metre for work that grows with size: tiling, painting, carpet cleaning, banner printing.
  • Per hour when you cannot tell in advance how long it takes: repairs where the fault is not yet known, video editing, design changes.
  • Per package for a fixed bundle with a clear scope: a half-day photo shoot, a 5-page website, a bridal makeup package.
  • Per month for ongoing work: tuition, website hosting, a weekly cleaning contract.

Keep 1 unit per line. If you sell the same work by the hour and as a package, make them 2 lines.

Fixed prices and "from" prices

A fixed price works when the job is the same every time. When it is not, you have 2 options:

  • Split the line by what changes the price: a salon can list "Rebonding, shoulder length" and "Rebonding, below shoulder" as 2 lines with 2 prices.
  • Use a "from" price when the difference only shows at the job, and say in the note what moves it: "Depends on hair length and colour."

Split the line when you can. A customer who sees "from" tends to expect the lowest number, so give the real price in the quotation before you start.

Put the small costs on the list

The costs people forget to charge are usually the small ones. Give each its own line:

  • Transport, parking and outstation trips
  • Disposal of old parts or rubbish
  • Materials you supply, such as brackets, pipes or props
  • Rush jobs, late nights and public holidays
  • A minimum charge per visit, for very short jobs

If you waive one of them for a customer, keep its line on the quotation at RM 0.00 instead of deleting it. The customer then sees what you included at no charge.

Your price list in Invois

In Invois, you build quotations from your price list.

  • Tap items from the list and set the quantity. Each line comes with your own price.
  • The note on an item prints under its name on the quotation.
  • Invois has no separate unit column, so when the unit is not obvious, put it in the name: "Hosting and maintenance, per month".
  • To set the list up, type the items, say them out loud, or photograph or upload the list you already have, even a whiteboard or an old quotation. AI reads the items, prices and headings, and you choose which to add.
  • Reading a list or adding items by voice uses 1 AI action each time: 15 AI actions a month on the free plan, or 30 a month after you verify your phone number. Reading your list when you first set up the app does not use the monthly allowance.

The price list itself has no limit on the free plan.

FAQ

Is a price list the same as a quotation?

No. A price list holds your usual rates for every customer. A quotation is the price of one job for one customer, with quantities, a total and a valid-until date. You build the quotation from the list.

Should I show my prices to customers?

If most of your jobs are standard, it saves time: customers see the price before they ask. If prices depend on the job, show the "from" prices and what moves them, then give the exact price in a quotation.

Per hour or per job: which is better?

Charge per job when you can see the whole job before you start, so the customer knows the total. Charge per hour when you cannot. You can also combine them: a package price, plus an hourly rate for anything beyond it.

Should my prices include SST?

If you are registered, it is simpler to keep the prices on your list before SST and add SST as its own line on the quotation. If you share the list, mark it "Prices exclude SST". If you are not registered, do not add SST at all. More in Do I need to charge SST?

How often should I update my price list?

Update it whenever one of your costs changes, such as a supplier's price or your transport costs. A quotation you have already sent normally keeps its price until its valid-until date, so new prices apply to new quotations.
