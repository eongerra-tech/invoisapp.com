Quotation vs invoice vs receipt: what's the difference in Malaysia?

The difference between a quotation, an invoice and a receipt in Malaysia, with one job followed from quote to deposit, balance and official receipt.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

4 min read · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short
  • A quotation offers a price before the job, an invoice asks for payment, and a receipt confirms you were paid.
  • Paid on the spot: a receipt, or an invoice marked PAID, is usually enough. Paid later, or by a company: send an invoice first, then a receipt for each payment.
  • One invoice can carry both the deposit and the balance; issue a receipt for each payment.

Quick test: if you have not asked for money yet, it is a quotation; if you are asking for money, it is an invoice; if the money has arrived, it is a receipt.

Suppose the headcount changes before the event: send a revised quotation or update the invoice before the balance is due. The deposit already paid still counts.

Not every job needs all three. A small job paid on the spot can skip the quotation, and a monthly fee such as tuition needs only an invoice each month and a receipt for each payment.

FAQ

Can a receipt replace an invoice?

For a small job paid on the spot, usually yes: many businesses give only a receipt, an invoice marked PAID or a cash bill. If the customer pays later or a company pays, send an invoice first, because that is what their accounts team pays against.

Should I give a receipt for a deposit?

Yes, for every payment, deposit included. Each receipt shows the balance still outstanding, so the customer sees what is left to pay.

Can I issue one invoice for the deposit and the balance?

Yes: list the deposit and the balance, each with its due date, as in step 2. If the customer's accounts team wants a separate invoice for each payment, issue a deposit invoice, then a final invoice that deducts the deposit.

How long should I keep invoices and receipts?

At least 7 years from the end of the year you earned the income; if a tax return is filed late, from the end of the year it is filed. This is general information, not tax advice.

Is an invoice the same as an e-Invoice?

No. e-Invoice is LHDN's system for submitting invoices to MyInvois, and businesses with annual turnover or revenue under RM 3,000,000 are currently exempt, with a few exceptions.

Sources

  • Keeping records for 7 years: Income Tax Act 1967, section 82; LHDN Public Rulings 4/2000 (Revised) and 5/2000 (Revised).
  • e-Invoice exemption: LHDN, e-Invoice Guideline, Version 4.8 (30 August 2026), section 1.6.1(e).
