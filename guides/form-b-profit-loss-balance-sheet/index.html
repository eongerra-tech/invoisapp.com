Your P&L and balance sheet on Form B: each e-B field in English

What each Malay field in the e-B P&L and balance sheet means, from Jualan atau perolehan to Baki akaun semasa hantar hadapan, with a freelancer's figures.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

5 min read · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short
  • e-B asks for a short P&L (profit and loss statement) and balance sheet of your main business, from your own accounts before tax adjustments. On the paper form this is Part N.
  • Fill it for 1 business only: if you have more than one, the one with the highest sales.
  • On e-B it sits under Profil Lain, with no N numbers, in Malay unless you switch e-B to English. Sales (Jualan atau perolehan) must be filled in.
  • Drawings (Ambilan) are what you took for yourself: they go in as a negative amount and reduce your current account.
Jump to:Where it is P&L Balance sheet Capital and drawings Balance check FAQ

The example is Daniel, a freelance software engineer with 1 business, Kod Daniel Enterprise, in his first year (2026). His expenses are worked out in business expenses you can claim on Form B.

Where to find it in e-B

  • In e-B, open Profil Lain, then "Maklumat Pendapatan Perniagaan dan Kewangan Orang Perseorangan", and click "Klik di sini untuk isi".
  • Enter whole ringgit, with no sen. Leave a field empty if it does not apply to you; Kos jualan takes 0 if you sell no stock.
  • The labels below are from Form B 2025. The e-B screen may word a few of them slightly differently.

Fixed assets go in at their net book value: cost less any depreciation in your accounts. Daniel's simple first-year accounts show them at cost. An accountant can prepare both statements for you.

Capital and drawings: what belongs to you

  • Akaun modal (capital account): the money you put into the business. Daniel put in RM 10,000 of savings.
  • Baki akaun semasa bawa hadapan (current account brought forward): last year's closing balance, so 0 in your first year.
  • Untung / rugi tahun semasa (this year's profit): the net profit above.
  • Ambilan / pendahuluan (Bersih) (drawings / advance, net): cash or stock you took for yourself. LHDN's notes make the carried-forward balance the sum of the lines above it, so drawings go in as a negative: tick "Tandakan 'X' jika negatif".
  • Baki akaun semasa hantar hadapan (current account carried forward): the result, which becomes next year's brought forward.

FAQ

Do I send my accounts with Form B?

No. LHDN says the records and working sheets need not be sent with the form, except for a tax refund, but you must keep them for 7 years after the year you file.

I have no SSM number. What do I enter?

In LHDN's e-B guide, you choose "Selain SSM", or "Others (Non-SSM)" on the English screen, as the registration type and leave the registration number empty.

I have 2 businesses. Which one goes in the P&L and balance sheet?

The one with the highest sales. The sales of the other business go in "Perniagaan lain", among the other income fields.
