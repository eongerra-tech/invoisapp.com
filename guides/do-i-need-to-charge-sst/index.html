Do I need to charge SST? A plain guide for self-employed people in Malaysia

Most self-employed people in Malaysia do not charge SST. When you must register (RM 500,000), which services are taxed at 8% or 6%, and what the invoice shows.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

5 min read · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short
  • Most self-employed people do not: you charge SST only once you are registered with Customs (JKDM).
  • You must register once your taxable services pass RM 500,000 in 12 months. Rental and construction work have higher limits.
  • Taxed at 8%: IT, consultancy, training, event management and repairs for businesses. Not taxed: renovating a house, cleaning homes, haircuts.
  • If you are not registered, do not put SST on your invoice: showing it is an offence.

Which services are taxed

You register once your taxable services pass RM 500,000 in 12 months, unless the table says otherwise.

What you doSST
Web development, programming, apps and other IT services8%
Consultancy, and paid training or coaching8%
Event management8%
Repair or maintenance for a shop, office or other business8%
Repairing movable items, such as a laptop or a TV, for anyone8%
Repairs to things fixed to a home, such as a leaking pipe, for the owner or occupantNo
Renovation, wiring or tiling on a houseNo
Construction work on shops, offices and factories6%, once over RM 1,500,000
Cleaning shops, offices or factories8%
Cleaning homesNo
Hair, nails and facialsNo (dropped in June 2025)
Renting out equipment, a studio or commercial space6%, once over RM 1,000,000

IT, consultancy, training, event management and repair services sit in one group, so their values are added together for the RM 500,000. A web developer who also teaches paid coding classes counts both.

Not spelled out by Customs

Customs has not given a clear answer for a few common jobs:

  • servicing an aircond in someone's home
  • private tutors who are not a registered tuition centre
  • photos or design made for advertising
  • makeup

If you are anywhere near RM 500,000 a year from one of these, ask the Customs Call Centre on 1-300-888-500 or at ccc@customs.gov.my before you decide.

How the RM 500,000 is counted

At the end of each month, look both ways. If either total is over RM 500,000, you must register:

  • Looking back: that month plus the 11 months before it.
  • Looking ahead: that month plus the next 11 months, if you have reason to expect them to pass it.

Only taxable services count. A plumber who mostly fixes pipes in homes does not count those jobs.

Suppose a freelance web developer bills about RM 5,000.00 a month:

Each month
RM 5,000.00
12 months
RM 60,000.00

That is far below RM 500,000, so the developer does not register and puts no SST on the invoice. If you do pass it, apply on MySST by the last day of the following month, and you charge SST from the first day of the month after you apply:

  1. End of JuneYour total passes RM 500,000
  2. By 31 JulyApply on MySST
  3. From 1 AugustCharge SST

If you are not registered

  • Do not add an SST line or show any amount as service tax. That is an offence, with a fine of up to RM 30,000, up to 2 years in jail, or both.
  • Any amount you showed as tax can also be collected from you.
  • If a customer asks for SST, tell them you are not SST-registered. You can still put your SSM number on the invoice.
  • If you provide taxable services, you may register voluntarily below the threshold, but then you charge SST and file returns, so talk to a tax agent first.

If you are registered

Your invoice has to show:

  • a serial number and the date
  • your name, address and SST registration number
  • a description of the service, and any discount
  • the total before tax, the rate, and the tax as a separate amount
  • the total including tax

Suppose a registered web developer builds a website:

RM 10,000.00

RM 800.00
Total
RM 10,800.00

For construction work, materials listed separately on the invoice are not taxed. The other things every invoice needs are in how to write an invoice. After that, the routine is:

  • Returns: form SST-02 covers 2 months and is filed, with payment, by the last day of the following month, even when there is nothing to pay.
  • When tax is due: when your customer pays you, not when you send the invoice; if they have not paid 12 months after the service, it is due anyway.
  • Records: keep them for 7 years.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Check your own case with Customs or a tax agent.

FAQ

Do hair salons and beauty services charge SST?

No. The plan to tax beauty services was dropped in June 2025, so haircuts, nails and facials are not taxed; massage parlours are a separate, taxable category.

Is SST the same as e-Invoice?

No. SST is a tax run by Customs. e-Invoice is LHDN's system for submitting invoices to MyInvois, and businesses with annual turnover or revenue under RM 3,000,000 are currently exempt, with a few exceptions.

Do I charge SST on a renovation for a house?

No, renovating a house is not taxed. Construction work on shops, offices and factories is taxed at 6%, once the contractor passes RM 1,500,000 in 12 months.

Is a service charge subject to SST?

For restaurants, no: Customs says the service charge is not subject to service tax, and SST is worked out on the price before it.
