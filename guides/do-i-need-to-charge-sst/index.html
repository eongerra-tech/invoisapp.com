Do I need to charge SST? A plain guide for self-employed people in Malaysia

Most self-employed people in Malaysia do not charge SST. When you must register (RM 500,000), which services are taxed at 8% or 6%, and what the invoice shows.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

8 min read · Last updated 23 October 2026 · General information, not tax advice

In short
  • You charge SST only if you are registered with Customs (JKDM).
  • You must register once your taxable services pass RM 500,000 in 12 months; for rental it is RM 1,000,000 and for construction work RM 1,500,000.
  • Renovation, wiring and tiling on a house, home cleaning and haircuts are not taxed.
  • If you are not registered, do not put SST on your invoice: showing it is an offence.

You charge service tax only if you are registered with the Royal Malaysian Customs Department, and you have to register only when your taxable services pass RM 500,000 in 12 months (higher for a few services).

Below that, you do not charge SST, and you must not show it on your invoice. Most freelancers, tradespeople and small studios are below that line.

Facts are as at 20 October 2026, from the law and Customs' own guides (links at the end).

3 checks

  1. Is what you do a taxable service? Only services listed in the Service Tax Regulations 2018 are taxed. Many everyday jobs are not on the list, or are excluded when done for a home.
  2. Have your taxable services passed the threshold? RM 500,000 in 12 months for most services; RM 1,000,000 for rental; RM 1,500,000 for construction work.
  3. Are you registered? Only a registered person charges service tax. If you are not registered, no SST goes on your invoice.

If the answer to 1 or 2 is no, you do not register and you do not charge SST.

In Invois, SST goes on its own line when you add it, and the SST number you enter once prints on your letterhead. More in SST in Invois.

Which services are taxed

What you do Taxed? Rate Register when over (12 months)
Web development, programming, apps and other IT services Yes 8% RM 500,000
Consultancy, and paid training or coaching (for example photography classes) Yes 8% RM 500,000
Event management Yes 8% RM 500,000
Repair or maintenance for a shop, office or other business Yes 8% RM 500,000
Repairing movable items, such as a laptop or a TV, for anyone, homes included Yes 8% RM 500,000
Repairing things fixed to a house, such as a blocked or leaking pipe, for the owner or occupant No – –
Renovation, wiring or tiling on a house No – –
Construction work on shops, offices and factories Yes 6% RM 1,500,000
Cleaning shops, offices or factories Yes 8% RM 500,000
Cleaning homes No – –
Hair, nails and facials No (dropped in June 2025) – –
Renting out equipment, a studio or commercial space (some small-business tenants are exempt) Yes 6% (from 1 January 2026) RM 1,000,000

IT, consultancy, training, event management and repair services all sit in one group, so their values are added together for the RM 500,000. A web developer who also teaches paid coding classes counts both.

Not spelled out by Customs

Customs has not given a clear answer for a few common jobs:

  • servicing an aircond in someone's home
  • private tutors who are not a registered tuition centre
  • photos or design made for advertising
  • makeup

If you are anywhere near RM 500,000 a year from one of these, ask Customs through MySST before you decide.

How the RM 500,000 is counted

You become liable at the end of a month when either is true, whichever comes first:

  • Looking back: that month plus the 11 months before it add up to more than RM 500,000.
  • Looking ahead: you have reason to expect that month plus the next 11 months to pass RM 500,000, for example because you have just signed a large contract.

Only taxable services count. A plumber who mostly fixes pipes in homes does not count those jobs.

Suppose a web developer bills every month. Add up 12 months:

  • RM 45,000.00 a month: 12 × RM 45,000.00 = RM 540,000.00, over RM 500,000, so they must register.
  • RM 30,000.00 a month: 12 × RM 30,000.00 = RM 360,000.00, not over, so they do not.

Once liable, apply on MySST by the last day of the following month. Registration starts on the first day of the month after you apply. Customs' own example: the threshold is passed in June, you apply in July, and you charge SST from 1 August.

If you are not registered

Do not add an SST line, and do not show any amount as service tax. Under section 21 of the Service Tax Act 2018 that is an offence, with a fine of up to RM 30,000, up to 2 years in jail, or both (section 79). Any amount you showed as tax can also be collected from you (section 28).

If a customer asks for SST on the invoice, the answer is that you are not registered, so the invoice has no SST. You can still put your SSM number on it.

You may register voluntarily below the threshold (section 14) if you provide taxable services, on conditions Customs sets. Once registered, you charge SST and file returns like any registered person, so talk to a tax agent first.

If you are registered

Your invoice has to show (Service Tax Regulations 2018, regulation 10):

  • a serial number and the date
  • your name, address and SST registration number
  • a description of the service, and any discount
  • the total before tax, the rate, and the tax as a separate amount
  • the total including tax

Suppose a registered web developer builds a website. The invoice reads:

  • Website: RM 10,000.00
  • SST 8%: 8% × RM 10,000.00 = RM 800.00
  • Total: RM 10,800.00

For construction work, materials listed separately on the invoice are not taxed. Suppose a registered contractor renovates a shop:

  • Materials: RM 6,000.00 (listed separately, not taxed)
  • Work: RM 4,000.00
  • SST 6%: 6% × RM 4,000.00 = RM 240.00
  • Total: RM 10,240.00

After that, the routine is:

  • Returns: form SST-02 covers 2 months and is filed, with payment, by the last day of the following month, even when there is nothing to pay.
  • When tax is due: when your customer pays you, not when you send the invoice. If they have not paid 12 months after the service, the tax is due anyway.
  • Records: keep them for 7 years.

The other things every invoice needs are in quotation vs invoice vs receipt.

SST in Invois

If you are registered:

  • When you make a quotation or invoice in Invois, add an SST line under Tax, at 8% or 6%. Or set it as a default tax in Document settings, so every new document has it.
  • SST is worked out after any discount, on its own line.
  • Enter your SST Reg No. in Company profile and it prints on your letterhead.
  • An invoice with SST is titled TAX INVOICE.

If you are not registered, leave SST off and the invoice shows no tax.

FAQ

I earn about RM 5,000 a month. Do I need to register?

No. That is about RM 60,000 a year (12 × RM 5,000.00), far below RM 500,000, so you do not register and you do not show SST on your invoices.

My customer wants SST on the invoice. What do I do?

If you are not registered, you cannot add it: showing service tax when you are not registered is an offence. Send the invoice without SST and tell the customer you are not SST-registered.

Do hair salons and beauty services charge SST?

No. The plan to tax beauty services from July 2025 was dropped in June 2025, so haircuts, nails and facials are not taxed. Massage parlours are a separate, taxable category.

Is SST the same as e-Invoice?

No. SST is a tax run by Customs. e-Invoice is LHDN's system for validating certain invoices, and whether it applies depends on your business. One does not replace the other.

Do I charge SST on a renovation for a house?

No. Renovation, wiring and tiling work on a house is not taxed. Construction work on shops, offices and factories is taxed at 6%, but only once the contractor's construction work passes RM 1,500,000 in 12 months and they register.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules change; check your own case with Customs on MySST or with a tax agent.
