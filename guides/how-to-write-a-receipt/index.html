How to write a receipt in Malaysia (with examples)

What to put on a receipt in Malaysia: receipt number, date paid, amount in words and balance, with 3 examples for a deposit, cash and 2 invoices paid at once.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

7 min read · Last updated 29 October 2026

In short
  • Put these on it: a receipt number, the date you were paid, who paid, the amount in figures and words, what it pays for, how it was paid, the balance left, and your business details.
  • When to issue it: once the money is in, for every payment: a deposit, each instalment, the balance, or cash paid on the spot.
  • If you sent a wrong receipt, do not change it. Mark it CANCELLED, keep it, and issue a new one with a new number.
  • Keep copies of your receipts for at least 7 years.

A receipt confirms that you received your customer's money: how much, when, and what it was for.

It usually follows an invoice (How to write an invoice). If you are not sure which document you need, see Quotation vs invoice vs receipt.

What to put on a receipt in Malaysia

1. Title and receipt number

Put RECEIPT or OFFICIAL RECEIPT at the top, with a receipt number. Never reuse a number, so each number points to one payment.

2. Date received

The date the money reached you, not the invoice date.

3. Your business details

Business name, address and phone. If you are registered with SSM, add your registration number: the rule covers invoices, bills and other documents that carry your business name.

References: Registration of Businesses Rules 1957, rule 13; Companies Act 2016.

4. Received from

The name of the person who paid, or the company name if a company paid.

5. What it pays for

The invoice number. With no invoice, a short description of the job and its date.

6. Amount received

The amount in figures and in words: RM 1,440.00 and "RINGGIT MALAYSIA ONE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY ONLY". The words make the amount harder to change.

7. How it was paid

Cash, bank transfer, DuitNow or cheque, with the transfer reference or cheque number.

8. Balance outstanding

What the customer still owes after this payment, or RM 0.00 when the invoice is fully paid.

In Invois, recording a payment on an invoice issues the receipt, with the customer, the invoice number and the balance taken from the invoice. More in Receipts in Invois.

One receipt for each payment

Issue a receipt every time money comes in, deposit included. A job paid as a deposit and a balance gets 2 receipts against 1 invoice.

Each receipt shows the money received that day, and the balance left after it. Issue it once the money is in, never before: a receipt says you have been paid.

Cash, transfers and cheques

  • Cash: write the receipt there and then, give the customer a copy and keep yours. For cash, your receipt is often the only record of the payment.
  • Bank transfer or DuitNow: check that the money is in your account, not only on the customer's screenshot, before you issue the receipt. Copy the transfer reference onto it.
  • Cheque: issue the receipt once the cheque has cleared, with the cheque number.

If a receipt is wrong

  • Not sent yet: just correct it.
  • Already sent: mark the wrong receipt CANCELLED and keep its copy. Issue a new receipt with a new number, and tell the customer which one it replaces.

Keeping the cancelled receipt means every number in your sequence is still accounted for.

How long to keep receipts

Keep your receipts, with your invoices and other business records, for at least 7 years from the end of the year the income relates to. This applies to sole proprietors, partnerships and companies alike.

If you are currently exempt from e-Invoice (businesses under RM 3,000,000 annual turnover or revenue, with a few exceptions explained in How to write an invoice), your receipts and other existing documents can be used by a business customer as proof of the expense for tax. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

References: Income Tax Act 1967, section 82; LHDN Public Rulings 4/2000 (Revised) and 5/2000 (Revised); LHDN, e-Invoice Guideline, Version 4.8 (30 August 2026), sections 1.6.1(e) and 1.6.2.

FAQ

Is a bank transfer slip enough as a receipt?

Not usually. The slip comes from the customer's bank and shows only that money was sent. Your receipt shows that you received it, which invoice it paid and what is still owed.

Can I write receipts by hand?

Yes. Use a receipt book with pre-numbered pages and a carbon copy, and keep the copies. Do not tear out a spoiled page: write CANCELLED across it.

Is an official receipt different from a receipt?

For a small business, no. OFFICIAL RECEIPT is the title many Malaysian businesses print on a numbered receipt. Customers and accountants look at what is on it, not at the title.

Can a receipt use the same number as the invoice?

Not the exact same number, because 1 invoice can have several receipts. A receipt number built from the invoice number, such as INV-2611-008-R1 for the first payment, keeps the 2 linked.
