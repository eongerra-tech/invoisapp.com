How to write a receipt in Malaysia (with examples)

What to put on a receipt in Malaysia: receipt number, date paid, amount in words and balance, with an example of a deposit and the balance.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

5 min read · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short
  • Put these on it: a receipt number, the date you were paid, who paid, the amount in figures and words, what it pays for, how it was paid, the balance left, and your business details.
  • When to issue it: every time money comes in, from a deposit or an instalment to the balance or cash on the spot.
  • If you sent a wrong receipt, do not change it. Mark it CANCELLED, keep it, and issue a new one with a new number.
  • Keep copies of your receipts for at least 7 years.

A receipt confirms that you received your customer's money: how much, when, and what it was for. It usually follows an invoice. Not sure which document you need? See quotation vs invoice vs receipt.

What to put on a receipt in Malaysia

  1. Title and receipt number. Put RECEIPT or OFFICIAL RECEIPT at the top, with a receipt number. Never reuse a number, so each number points to one payment.
  2. Date received. The date the money reached you, not the invoice date.
  3. Your business details. Business name, address and phone. If you are registered with SSM, the law requires your registration number on it.
  4. Received from. The name of the person who paid, or the company name if a company paid.
  5. What it pays for. The invoice number. With no invoice, describe the job and its date, such as "House cleaning, 4 hours, 14 November 2026"; a cash bill also works. If 1 transfer pays 2 invoices, list each invoice number with the amount paid against it.
  6. Amount received. In figures and in words, such as RM 1,440.00 and "RINGGIT MALAYSIA ONE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY ONLY". The words make the amount harder to change.
  7. How it was paid. Cash, bank transfer, DuitNow or cheque, with the transfer reference or cheque number.
  8. Balance outstanding. What the customer still owes after this payment, or RM 0.00 when the invoice is fully paid. A deposit and a balance mean 2 receipts for 1 invoice.

Example: a deposit receipt

Farah Photography's invoice INV-2611-008 is RM 4,800.00, with a 30% deposit to book the date. The customer transfers the deposit on 5 November 2026:

Cash, transfers and cheques

  • Cash: write the receipt there and then, give the customer a copy and keep yours. For cash, your receipt is often the only record of the payment.
  • Bank transfer or DuitNow: check that the money is in your account, not only on the customer's screenshot, before you issue the receipt. Copy the transfer reference onto it.
  • Cheque: issue the receipt once the cheque has cleared, with the cheque number.

If a receipt is wrong

  • Not sent yet: just correct it.
  • Already sent: mark the wrong receipt CANCELLED and keep its copy. Issue a new receipt with a new number, and tell the customer which one it replaces.

How long to keep receipts

Keep your receipts, with your invoices and other business records, for at least 7 years from the end of the year you earned the income. Keep the receipts for your business expenses too: see business expenses on Form B. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

FAQ

Is a bank transfer slip enough as a receipt?

Not usually. The slip only shows that the customer sent money; your receipt shows that you received it, which invoice it paid and what is still owed.

Can I write receipts by hand?

Yes. Use a receipt book with pre-numbered pages and a carbon copy, and keep the copies. Do not tear out a spoiled page: write CANCELLED across it.

Is an official receipt different from a receipt?

For a small business, no. OFFICIAL RECEIPT is the title many Malaysian businesses print on a numbered receipt, and customers and accountants look at what is on it, not at the title.

Can a receipt use the same number as the invoice?

Not the exact same number, because 1 invoice can have several receipts. A receipt number built from the invoice number, such as INV-2611-008-R1 for the first payment, keeps the 2 linked.

My business customer wants an e-Invoice. Is my receipt enough?

If you are currently exempt from e-Invoice (annual turnover or revenue under RM 3,000,000, with a few exceptions), your receipts and invoices can be used as the customer's proof of expense (see e-Invoice in how to write an invoice).

Sources

  • SSM number on receipts: Registration of Businesses Rules 1957, rule 13; Companies Act 2016.
  • Keeping records for 7 years: Income Tax Act 1967, section 82; LHDN Public Rulings 4/2000 (Revised) and 5/2000 (Revised).
  • e-Invoice exemption and proof of expense: LHDN, e-Invoice Guideline, Version 4.8 (30 August 2026), sections 1.6.1(e) and 1.6.2.
