Cash sales vs invoice: what is a cash bill?

A cash bill records a sale paid in full on the spot: the bill and the receipt in one. Here is when to use one instead of an invoice and what to put on it.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

6 min read · Last updated 3 November 2026

In short
  • A cash sales bill (or cash bill) records a sale paid in full at the time of sale. It is the bill and the receipt in one.
  • Which to use: a cash bill when the customer pays before they leave; an invoice when they pay later or in parts.
  • A cash bill needs: a running number, the date, your business details, what was sold, the total and how it was paid.
  • Records: keep copies for 7 years. Once your yearly takings pass RM 150,000 from goods or RM 100,000 from services, receipts must be numbered in series.

Cash bill or invoice?

Both record a sale. The difference is when the money comes in.

Cash billInvoice
PaymentIn full, at the time of saleLater, or in parts
IssuedAfter the customer paysBefore the customer pays, with a due date
ThenNothing more to sendA receipt for each payment
Common forWalk-in customers, small jobs paid at the doorBigger jobs, deposits, company customers

"Cash" means paid in full on the spot, not only notes and coins. A sale paid by DuitNow, card or bank transfer before the customer leaves is a cash sale too.

Some businesses print CASH SALES or CASH BILL at the top. Others issue an invoice marked PAID, with a receipt. Both record the same sale, so pick one way and keep to it.

What to put on a cash bill

  • CASH SALES or CASH BILL at the top, with a running number.
  • The date of the sale.
  • Your business name, address and phone, and your SSM number if you are registered.
  • The customer's name, when you know it. A company customer will also want its own name and address on it.
  • What was sold: each item or service, the quantity, the price and the total.
  • How it was paid: cash, DuitNow, card or transfer, with the reference.

If you are registered for SST, a cash bill for a taxable service needs the details an SST invoice has: see If you are registered.

References: Registration of Businesses Rules 1957, rule 13(2); Service Tax Regulations 2018, regulation 10.

Example: a tailor's cash bill

A customer picks up 2 pairs of trousers that were hemmed and a dress that was taken in, and pays by DuitNow at the counter.

Trousers hemmed, 2 × RM 15.00
RM 30.00
Dress taken in
RM 45.00
Total, paid by DuitNow
RM 75.00

The cash bill shows the 2 lines, the total, "Paid by DuitNow" and the transfer reference. Nothing is owed, so no invoice and no separate receipt follow.

When the customer pays only part

A deposit or a part payment is not a cash sale, because money is still owed. Issue an invoice for the full amount, then a receipt for each payment, as in how to write a receipt.

Records

  • Keep a copy of every cash bill for 7 years, with your other records.
  • Number your receipts in series. Once your yearly takings pass RM 150,000 from selling goods or RM 100,000 from services, this is required.
  • A small business, with takings up to those amounts, may keep a cash book of bank entries, cash received and cash paid out.
  • Write each sale into your books as soon as you can, and within 60 days.

References: Income Tax Act 1967, section 82; LHDN Public Ruling No. 5/2000 (Revised), paragraphs 3.3.1 and 4.2.

Cash sales and e-Invoice

With an annual turnover or revenue of less than RM 3,000,000, you are currently exempt from e-Invoice, with a few exceptions explained in how to write an invoice. For tax, a business customer can use your cash bills as proof of the expense.

At RM 3,000,000 or more, a customer who asks for an e-Invoice gets one. Sales to customers who do not ask can go into 1 consolidated e-Invoice a month, submitted within 7 calendar days after the month ends.

Some activities, and any single sale above RM 10,000, need an e-Invoice each time: see Table 3.6 of LHDN's Specific Guideline.

In Invois, a sale paid on the spot is an invoice with the payment recorded at once, which issues the receipt. More in Cash sales in Invois.

FAQ

What is a cash sales invoice?

The same thing as a cash bill. Some accounting software calls it a cash sales invoice because it lists the items like an invoice but is issued already paid.

Is a cash bill the same as an official receipt?

Both show money received. A cash bill also lists what was sold, so for a sale paid in full it does the job of the invoice and the receipt. See how to write a receipt for what a receipt needs.

Do I need the customer's name on a cash bill?

Not for a walk-in customer who pays in full. A company customer usually needs its name and address on it to claim the expense, so ask before you print it.

The customer paid in cash but wants an invoice. What do I do?

Issue the invoice and mark it paid, or issue the invoice with a receipt dated the same day. Do not issue a cash bill as well, or the sale is recorded twice.

Can I cancel a cash bill?

Do not delete or reuse its number. Mark the copy CANCELLED, keep it, and issue a new cash bill with the next number.
