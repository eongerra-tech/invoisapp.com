How to file Form B: a step-by-step guide for your first year

If you work for yourself in Malaysia, here is how to file Form B on e-Filing step by step, with the deadline, the accounts it asks for and the penalties for filing late.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

10 min read · Last updated 4 November 2026

In short
  • Who files Form B: anyone resident in Malaysia with business income, including freelance work and a side business next to a job. Residents without business income file Form BE.
  • When: by 30 June of the year after the income. For 2025 income, LHDN accepted e-Filing and payment until 15 July 2026.
  • How: online only, as e-B on MyTax. The first time, you get a PIN online with your MyKad.
  • Late: a penalty of 15% to 45% of the tax, plus 10% on tax paid late.

Citizens and permanent residents aged 18 and over get a tax number (TIN) automatically. You do not need to register a new tax file for the business.

References: Income Tax Act 1967, section 77(1); LHDN, Pendaftaran (TIN registration), updated 15 June 2026.

When to file

The year of assessment is the year the income was earned, so Daniel's 2026 income is year of assessment 2026. His dates are:

  1. 31 December 2026The year he reports ends
  2. From March 2027e-B opens (it opened on 1 March in 2026)
  3. 30 June 2027Due date for the form and the tax
  4. 15 July 2027Last day of the grace period for e-Filing and payment, if LHDN gives the same 15 days as in 2026

The grace period is set each year in LHDN's filing programme. A form sent after it counts as late from 1 July.

References: Income Tax Act 1967, sections 77(1)(a) and 77(1B); LHDN Return Form Filing Programme for the Year 2026 (updated 1 April 2026), Guide Note 1, (iii) and Example 2.

Step 2: Log in to MyTax

The first time, get a PIN online in one of 2 ways:

WayHow
e-KYCOn the MyTax phone app: a selfie matched to the photo on your MyKad
e-CP55DOn the MyTax website, from a computer

With the PIN you set a password and register a digital certificate, which you need to send the form. The certificate lasts 3 years.

Then open e-B:

  1. Go to mytax.hasil.gov.my and log in.
  2. Choose Perkhidmatan ezHasil, then e-Filing, then e-Borang.
  3. Next to e-B ("Ada Punca Pendapatan Perniagaan"), pick the Tahun Taksiran. Daniel picks 2026.

References: LHDN, e-Perkhidmatan, "Permohonan No. PIN" (updated 8 September 2026); LHDN slides "e-Filing Borang e-B", Tax Education Division (29 March 2022), digital certificate.

Step 3: Fill in e-B, part by part

e-B has 5 parts, listed down the left of the screen. It takes whole ringgit, so the amounts in Steps 3 and 4 have no sen.

PartWhat goes in
1. Profil IndividuMarital status, type of assessment, whether you keep records. Daniel: single, records kept.
2. Profil LainPhone, email, business address and refund account. From here you also open the business pages: losses and your accounts (Step 4).
3. PendapatanStatutory income from business and the number of businesses, any salary, rent or dividends, and donations. Daniel: RM 77,546 from 1 business.
4. PelepasanPersonal reliefs. Daniel: RM 9,000 for himself and RM 4,000 for EPF.
5. RumusanThe tax, worked out for you (Step 5).

Enter 0 where you have nothing. Most self-employed people leave the foreign income, incentive and prior-year income pages empty.

References: LHDN Borang B 2025, Parts A, B, D, H, M and N; LHDN slides "e-Filing Borang e-B" (29 March 2022), Halaman 1 to 5.

Daniel got no CP500 for 2026, so the whole year's tax is paid with the form. In later years, LHDN may send a CP500 notice asking you to pay the tax in advance, in 6 instalments every 2 months from March. Chargeable income of RM 35,000 or less also gets a RM 400 rebate.

Step 6: Submit and pay

  1. Check the summary, read the declaration and click Tandatangan & Hantar (sign and send).
  2. Save the acknowledgement slip. It carries the bill number (Nombor Bil) you pay with.
  3. Pay by the due date at ByrHASiL, at an appointed bank, or at a Pos Malaysia counter.

You do not attach receipts or worksheets. Keep them for 7 years in case LHDN asks.

References: LHDN Borang B 2025, page 1, notes 6 and 7; LHDN Explanatory Notes Form B 2025, page 1; Income Tax Act 1967, section 82.

If you file late or make a mistake

Form sent late byPenalty on the tax
Up to 12 months15%
Over 12 and up to 24 months30%
Over 24 months45%

Tax not paid by the end of the grace period also goes up by 10%.

Found a mistake after sending? If it means more tax, send an amended return on MyTax (e-BNT) after the due date and within 6 months of it. You can do this once, and the extra tax goes up by 10%. For any other mistake, call the HASiL Contact Centre on 03-8911 1000.

Not filing at all is worse: LHDN can estimate your tax and add a 45% penalty, or take you to court.

This is general information, not tax advice.

References: LHDN Operational Guideline No. 3/2020, paragraphs 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 4.1 and 4.3; LHDN Borang B 2025, page 1, note 9; Income Tax Act 1967, sections 77B, 103(3), 112(1) and 112(3); LHDN, e-Perkhidmatan, e-BNT.

FAQ

Do I have to file if my income is low?

You must file for a year in which you have chargeable income. You must also file if you had chargeable income or filed a return the year before, even when this year has none. A business loss is reported on the form, in Part M, and can be carried forward for up to 10 years in a row.

I have a job and a side business. Do I file 2 forms?

No. You file 1 Form B, with the salary from your EA form and the business profit on it. The tax your employer deducted each month comes off at the end.

Do I need an accountant to file Form B?

No. You can file e-B yourself. The tax agent section is only for an agent who files for you, but an accountant can help with the accounts in Part N.

Can I ask LHDN for more time?

LHDN's online extension service, e-Lanjutan Masa, covers companies, co-operatives, LLPs and trust bodies, not individuals. For individuals, the extra time is the 15-day e-Filing grace period in LHDN's filing programme.
