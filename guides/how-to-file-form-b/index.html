How to file Form B: a step-by-step guide for your first year

If you work for yourself in Malaysia, here is how to file Form B on e-Filing step by step, with the deadline, the accounts it asks for and the penalties for filing late.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

7 min read · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short
  • Who files Form B: residents with business income, including freelance work and a side business. Residents without business income file Form BE.
  • When: by 30 June of the following year, so 30 June 2027 for 2026 income. In 2026, LHDN allowed e-Filing and payment until 15 July.
  • How: online only. On MyTax, choose Perkhidmatan ezHasil, then e-Filing, e-Borang and e-B. The first time, you get a PIN online with your MyKad.
  • Late: a penalty of 15% to 45% of the tax, plus 10% on tax paid late.

The example is Daniel, a freelance software engineer filing his first Form B in 2027. His statutory income for 2026 is RM 77,546.00, the profit after capital allowances from the business expenses guide.

Form B or Form BE?

Form B covers business income, including freelance work. With a salary and a side business, you file 1 Form B with both on it; with only a salary, you file Form BE.

Citizens and permanent residents aged 18 and over get a tax number (TIN) automatically, so you do not register a new tax file for the business.

When to file

The year of assessment is the year the income was earned: Daniel's 2026 income is year of assessment 2026. His dates:

  1. From March 2027e-B opens (it opened on 1 March in 2026)
  2. 30 June 2027Due date for the form and the tax
  3. 15 July 2027Last day of the grace period, if LHDN repeats the 15 days of 2026

The grace period is set each year in LHDN's filing programme. A form sent after it counts as late from 1 July.

Step 2: Log in to MyTax

The first time, get a PIN on the MyTax website:

  1. At mytax.hasil.gov.my, choose MyKad as the ID type, enter your IC number and click Hantar (Submit).
  2. A box says Sijil Digital tidak wujud (no digital certificate yet). Choose e-CP55D and click Ya if the email shown is yours, then check your name and email and click Hantar.
  3. LHDN emails you an activation link: open it within 2 days. Your PIN is already filled in, so enter your IC number and click Hantar.
  4. Create a password (Kata Laluan) and a security phrase (Frasa Keselamatan), tick Setuju and click Hantar. This also registers your digital certificate, valid for 3 years.
  • No email on file? Add one and upload a colour copy of your MyKad for LHDN to check.
  • On the MyTax phone app, use Daftar Kali Pertama instead (e-KYC): you photograph both sides of your MyKad and take a selfie.

Then open e-B:

  1. To log in, enter your IC number, click Hantar, check the security phrase is yours, then enter your password and click Log Masuk.
  2. Choose Perkhidmatan ezHasil, then e-Filing, then e-Borang.
  3. Next to e-B ("Ada Punca Pendapatan Perniagaan"), pick the Tahun Taksiran. Daniel picks 2026.

To see e-B in English, click EN at the top right.

Step 3: Fill in e-B, part by part

e-B has 5 parts, listed down the left of the screen. Amounts are in whole ringgit, with no sen.

PartWhat goes in
1. Profil IndividuMarital status, type of assessment, whether you keep records. Daniel: single, records kept.
2. Profil LainPhone, email, business address and refund account. The business pages, including your accounts (Step 4), open from here.
3. PendapatanStatutory income from business, any salary, rent or dividends, and donations. Daniel: RM 77,546 from 1 business.
4. PelepasanPersonal reliefs: see tax relief for the self-employed. Daniel: RM 9,000 for himself and RM 4,000 for EPF.
5. RumusanThe tax, worked out for you (Step 5).

Leave a field empty if it does not apply. Most self-employed people leave the foreign, incentive and prior-year income pages empty.

Step 4: Enter your P&L and balance sheet

e-B also asks for your main business's P&L (profit and loss statement) and balance sheet, before tax adjustments (Part N on paper). An accountant can prepare them.

Each field, with its Malay label and Daniel's figures: see your P&L and balance sheet in e-B. Part 3 takes your statutory income (RM 77,546 for Daniel), not the net profit in the P&L (RM 79,520).

Daniel got no CP500 for 2026, so he pays the whole year's tax with the form. Later, LHDN may send a CP500 notice to pay tax in advance, in 6 instalments every 2 months from March.

Chargeable income of RM 35,000 or less also gets a RM 400 rebate.

Step 6: Submit and pay

  1. On the AKUAN (declaration) page, tick Agree if e-B asks you to confirm your total income. Click Tandatangan & hantar (Sign and Submit in English), enter your IC number and MyTax password, and click Tandatangan.
  2. Save the slip (Cetak Pengesahan). It shows your bill number (Nombor Bil) and the tax to pay.
  3. Pay by the due date at ByrHASiL: choose Bayaran Menggunakan Nombor Bil, enter the 16-digit bill number and the security code, click Teruskan, check the amount and pay by FPX.
  • ByrHASiL takes online banking (FPX) only. You can also pay at Pos Malaysia, or at an appointed bank's counter, CDM, ATM or internet banking.
  • Lost the bill number? In MyTax, open Perkhidmatan ezHasil, then e-Billing, then Paparan Bil Pembayar Cukai.
  • You do not attach receipts or worksheets. Keep them for 7 years in case LHDN asks.

If you file late or make a mistake

Form sent late byPenalty on the tax
Up to 12 months15%
Over 12 and up to 24 months30%
Over 24 months45%

Tax not paid by the end of the grace period also goes up by 10%.

Found a mistake after sending? If it means more tax, send an amended return on MyTax (e-BNT) within 6 months after the due date. You can do this once, and the extra tax goes up by 10%.

For any other mistake, call the HASiL Contact Centre on 03-8911 1000.

Not filing at all is worse: LHDN can estimate your tax, add a 45% penalty or take you to court.

This is general information, not tax advice.

FAQ

Do I have to file if my income is low?

You must file for any year with chargeable income, and for the year right after one with chargeable income or a filed return, even with none this year. A business loss still goes on the form (Part M).

I have a job and a side business. Do I file 2 forms?

No. You file 1 Form B, with the salary from your EA form and the business profit on it. The tax your employer deducted each month comes off at the end.

Can I ask LHDN for more time?

No. LHDN's online extension service, e-Lanjutan Masa, is not for individuals; your extra time is the 15-day e-Filing grace period.
