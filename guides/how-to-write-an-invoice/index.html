How to write an invoice in Malaysia (with examples)

What to put on an invoice in Malaysia: number, due date, SSM no., SST and payment details, with 3 examples and when e-Invoice applies.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

8 min read · Last updated 23 October 2026

In short
  • Put these on it: an invoice number, the date and due date, your business details, the customer's details, the items, the total, any deposit paid, and your bank details.
  • Send it as soon as payment is due: payment terms count from the invoice date.
  • If you sent a wrong invoice, send a corrected one and say what changed. If you are SST-registered, mark the wrong one CANCELLED instead; a later change in value goes on a credit or debit note.
  • Businesses under RM 3,000,000 annual turnover or revenue are currently exempt from e-Invoice, with a few exceptions.

An invoice asks your customer to pay a set amount by a set date.

The invoice usually follows a quotation (How to write a quotation). If you are not sure which document you need, see Quotation vs invoice vs receipt.

What to put on an invoice in Malaysia

1. Title and invoice number

Put the word INVOICE at the top, with a number such as INV-2611-012. Number invoices in order and never reuse a number, so each number points to one invoice.

2. Invoice date and due date

Write the invoice date and the due date, using the payment terms from your quotation. Terms such as "14 days" or "Net 30" work, but a date (10 December 2026) leaves less room for argument.

3. Your business details

Business name, address, phone and email. If you are registered with SSM, add your registration number: sole proprietors, partnerships and companies must all show it on their invoices.

References: Registration of Businesses Rules 1957, rule 13; Companies Act 2016.

4. Who you are billing

Customer name, plus the company name and address if a company is paying. Add the customer's PO (purchase order) number if they gave you one.

The company's accounts team matches your invoice to the order by that number, so leaving it off can hold up payment.

5. Item lines

The same items, quantities and prices as the accepted quotation. Keep free items on the invoice at RM 0.00.

If the job changed, give each change its own line, so the customer can see why the total differs from the quotation.

6. Totals and SST

Subtotal, discount (if any) and total, in RM with 2 decimals.

  • Registered with JKDM for SST: SST goes on its own line with its rate, and your SST registration number goes on the invoice.
  • Not registered: do not add SST.

More in Do I need to charge SST?

7. Deposit and balance

If the customer paid a deposit, show it under the total, then the balance still due, so both of you can see the deposit was counted.

8. How to pay

Bank name, account name and account number, or a DuitNow QR. Ask the customer to put the invoice number in the transfer reference, so you know which invoice the money is for.

Invois prints all 8 on the invoice, and you enter your business and bank details only once. More in Invoices in Invois.

When to send an invoice

  • After the job, for work paid on completion.
  • Before you start, for a deposit, if the customer wants an invoice to pay it.
  • At each stage of a longer job, as agreed in the quotation (for example 50% to start and 50% on completion).
  • Once a month, for ongoing work such as tuition or a maintenance contract.

Send it as soon as payment is due. Terms such as "14 days" count from the invoice date, so a late invoice is paid later.

Tiler: extra work the customer agreed to

Quotation Q-2610-022 was RM 2,130.00 to retile a bathroom. Under the old tiles the floor trap was cracked, and on 20 October the customer agreed on WhatsApp to have it replaced for RM 80.00. Invoice INV-2610-031, dated 24 October 2026, is due on receipt.

If an invoice is wrong

  • Not sent yet: just correct it.
  • Already sent: send the corrected invoice and tell the customer what changed.

SST-registered businesses have stricter rules. An issued invoice must not be altered: a wrong one is marked CANCELLED and kept for audit. A later change in value goes on a credit note (for less) or a debit note (for more).

References: JKDM, MySST, Issuing invoices.

Do you need e-Invoice?

e-Invoice is LHDN's system for submitting invoices through MyInvois for validation. LHDN has phased it in since 1 August 2024, starting with businesses with an annual turnover or revenue of more than RM 100 million.

Businesses with an annual turnover or revenue of less than RM 3,000,000 are currently exempt from issuing e-Invoices, whether they are individuals, partnerships or companies.

The exemption does not apply if your business has a shareholder that is not an individual (such as a company), a holding company, a related company or a joint venture with an annual turnover or revenue of at least RM 3,000,000.

If you are exempt, you keep issuing normal invoices and receipts, and you can still choose to join e-Invoice. LHDN reviews the exemptions from time to time, so check the current guideline before you rely on this. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

References: LHDN, e-Invoice Guideline, Version 4.8 (30 August 2026), sections 1.5, 1.6.1(e), 1.6.6, 1.6.8 and 1.6.10.

FAQ

What does Net 30 mean?

Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. That is not always the same date next month: an invoice dated 15 January on Net 30 is due on 14 February.

Can I send an invoice without a quotation?

Yes. Many people invoice small jobs, repeat work and monthly fees directly. The item lines then have to explain the job on their own.

Can I put several jobs on one invoice?

Yes, if they are for the same customer and paid together, such as a month of jobs for one company. Give each job its own lines with its date or location, so the customer can check them one by one.

What should I do if a customer pays late?

Send a short reminder with the invoice number, the amount and the due date, and attach the invoice again. If it is still unpaid, send a second reminder, and hold off on new work for that customer until it is paid. Interest on late payment is covered in Quotation terms and conditions.

My customer asked for an e-Invoice. What should I do?

If you are exempt, you do not have to issue one: your normal receipts and documents are used as the customer's proof of expense. If you are not exempt, you submit e-Invoices through MyInvois, on LHDN's portal or through software that connects to it (LHDN e-Invoice Guideline, sections 1.6.2, 2.3 and 2.4).
