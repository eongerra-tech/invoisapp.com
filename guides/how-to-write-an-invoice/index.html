How to write an invoice in Malaysia (with examples)

What to put on an invoice in Malaysia: number, due date, SSM no., SST and payment details, with an example and when e-Invoice applies.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

5 min read · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short
  • Put these on it: an invoice number, the date and due date, your business details, the customer's details, the items, the total, any deposit paid, and how to pay you.
  • When to send it: after the job, for a deposit, at each payment stage, or once a month.
  • Wrong invoice: send a corrected one and say what changed. If you are SST-registered, mark the wrong one CANCELLED instead.
  • e-Invoice: businesses with annual turnover or revenue under RM 3,000,000 are currently exempt, with a few exceptions.

An invoice asks your customer to pay a set amount by a set date. It usually follows a quotation. Not sure which document you need? See quotation vs invoice vs receipt.

What to put on an invoice in Malaysia

  1. Invoice number. Write INVOICE at the top and give it a number, such as INV-2611-003. Number them in order and never reuse one.
  2. Invoice date and due date. Use the payment terms you agreed, usually in your quotation. A date ("due 10 December 2026") leaves less room for argument than "14 days".
  3. Your business details. Name, address, phone and email. If you are registered with SSM, the law requires your registration number on the invoice (do I need to register?).
  4. The customer. Their name, and the company name and address if a company pays. Add their PO (purchase order) number if they gave one: their accounts team uses it to match and pay your invoice.
  5. The items. The same items, quantities and prices as the accepted quotation. Keep free items at RM 0.00. If the job changed, put each change on its own line (for example "Extra: 1 more round of logo changes"), so the customer sees why the total differs from the quotation.
  6. Total and SST. Subtotal, discount and total in RM with 2 decimals. If you are registered with JKDM for SST, show SST on its own line with its rate and add your SST registration number. If not, do not add SST (do I need to charge SST?).
  7. Deposit and balance. If the customer paid a deposit, show it under the total, then the balance still due. Each payment gets its own receipt.

  8. How to pay you. Bank name, account name and number, or a DuitNow QR. Ask the customer to put the invoice number in the transfer reference.

When to send an invoice

  • After the job, for work paid on completion.
  • Before you start, for a deposit, if the customer wants an invoice to pay it.
  • At each stage of a longer job, as agreed in the quotation (for example 50% to start and 50% on completion).
  • Once a month, for ongoing work such as tuition or a maintenance contract.

Send it as soon as payment is due. Terms such as "14 days" count from the invoice date, so a late invoice gets paid later.

If an invoice is wrong

  • Not sent yet: just correct it.
  • Already sent: send the corrected invoice and tell the customer what changed.
  • SST-registered: an issued invoice must not be altered. Mark the wrong one CANCELLED and keep it, and put a later change in value on a credit note (for less) or a debit note (for more).

What about e-Invoice?

e-Invoice is LHDN's system for submitting each invoice to MyInvois.

  • Under RM 3,000,000 a year in turnover or revenue: you are currently exempt, so keep issuing normal invoices.
  • Not exempt if your business has one of these with RM 3,000,000 or more:
    • a shareholder that is not an individual, such as a company;
    • a holding company or a related company;
    • a joint venture.

LHDN reviews the exemptions from time to time, so check the current guideline. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

FAQ

What does Net 30 mean?

Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date, which is not always the same date next month: an invoice dated 15 January on Net 30 is due on 14 February.

Can I send an invoice without a quotation?

Yes. Many people invoice small jobs, repeat work and monthly fees directly. Without a quotation, the item lines have to explain the job on their own.

What should I do if a customer pays late?

Send a short reminder with the invoice number, the amount and the due date, and attach the invoice again. If it stays unpaid, see customer not paying.

My customer asked for an e-Invoice. What should I do?

If you are exempt, you do not have to issue one: your normal invoices and receipts can be used as the customer's proof of expense. If you are not exempt, you submit e-Invoices through MyInvois.

What format should an invoice number use?

Any format, as long as the numbers run in order and each is used once. A date part helps: INV-2611-012 is invoice 12 of November 2026.
