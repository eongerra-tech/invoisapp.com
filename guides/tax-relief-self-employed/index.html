Tax relief for the self-employed: what you can claim on Form B

Which personal reliefs you can claim on Form B for 2026 when you work for yourself in Malaysia: EPF up to RM 7,000, what is not covered, and what is new.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

5 min read · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short
  • Reliefs come off after your business profit: profit minus reliefs is the income you pay tax on.
  • Everyone gets RM 9,000 for themselves. Most other reliefs are the same as for employees: lifestyle, medical, insurance, spouse and children.
  • Voluntary EPF, such as i-Saraan, counts up to RM 7,000 if you pay no life insurance.
  • Not covered for 2026: SOCSO's self-employment scheme. A computer or phone you use for work is a business cost, not lifestyle relief.

This page is for the year of assessment 2026: the income of 2026, filed on Form B in 2027.

How reliefs work on Form B

First work out your business profit: see business expenses you can claim on Form B. Then:

  • Your reliefs go in Part H of Form B, and their total comes off the profit.
  • What is left is your chargeable income. The first RM 5,000 is taxed at 0%, and the rates rise from 1% in steps.
  • If your chargeable income is RM 35,000 or less, you also get a RM 400 rebate off the tax.

EPF: up to RM 7,000 if you pay it yourself

With no employer paying EPF for you, you can pay in yourself, for example through i-Saraan. Form B counts it in 2 parts:

EPF, voluntary or compulsory (Form B H17(ii))
RM 4,000
More EPF, or life insurance (H17(i))
+ RM 3,000
Most you can claim
RM 7,000

Life insurance shares the second part. If you pay RM 2,000 of life insurance, only RM 1,000 more of EPF counts there.

What is not covered

  • SOCSO's self-employment scheme. The SOCSO relief (RM 350) covers only the employee schemes for 2026. Budget 2027 proposes covering it from 2027, within the RM 350 for 3 compulsory sectors plus RM 150 for voluntary contributions; it is not law yet.
  • A computer, phone or tablet you use for the business. Claim it in the business accounts as capital allowance instead, as in Step 3 of the business expenses guide.

New for 2026

  • Entrance fees to tourist attractions and arts programmes: up to RM 1,000, for 2026 only (Budget 2027 proposes 1 more year).
  • Child care: now also at registered care centres, for a child aged 12 or under.
  • Support for a child with a learning disability: up to RM 10,000, from RM 6,000, within the medical relief.
  • Life insurance on your child's life now counts.
  • The RM 2,500 relief for EV charging now also covers a food waste grinder and CCTV for your home (once, in 2026 or 2027).

Budget 2027 proposes raising the relief for yourself to RM 12,000 from 2027. It applies only once passed into law.

FAQ

I paid more than RM 7,000 into EPF. Can I claim it all?

No. RM 7,000 is the most for EPF and life insurance together; the rest gets no relief, though it stays your savings.

Why does LHDN's relief page still show 2025?

As at November 2026, LHDN's page (updated 26 June 2026) still lists 2025, the last year filed. The 2026 amounts on this page come from the Income Tax Act as amended by the Finance Act 2025.

Does the RM 400 rebate come back as a refund?

No. It only reduces the tax you owe; if your tax is less than RM 400, the rest is not paid out.
