CP500: what it is, when to pay and how to change it

CP500 is LHDN's notice to pay your income tax in 6 instalments. Here is who gets it, the due dates, how to pay at ByrHASiL and how to revise it with CP502.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

6 min read · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short
  • CP500 is LHDN's notice to pay this year's income tax in advance, in 6 instalments: every 2 months from March to the next January.
  • It goes to people with income other than a salary, such as a business, rent or royalties. LHDN bases the amount on your tax for the year before.
  • Pay each instalment at ByrHASiL by its due date. One still unpaid 30 days later goes up by 10%.
  • To change the amount, send form CP502 by 30 June, by 31 October, or both.

What CP500 is

An employee's tax comes off their pay every month through PCB. When you work for yourself, LHDN may send a CP500 (Notis Bayaran Ansuran) instead.

  • Who gets it: people with income other than a salary, such as a business, rent or royalties. If you also have a salary, the tax on it is left out, because PCB covers it.
  • How much: LHDN's estimate, based on your tax for the year before.
  • When it comes: LHDN sends CP500 notices by February. If yours has not come by the end of February, look under Status MyTax > CP500 in MyTax, or call the HASiL Contact Centre on 03-8911 1000.

When to pay: 6 instalments

Each due date is the 1st of the month, as below. You have 30 days after each due date to pay.

Take Daniel, the freelance software engineer from our Form B guides. He got no CP500 for 2026, his first year. Suppose a later CP500 asks him for RM 3,000.00 for the year, in 6 instalments of RM 500.00:

  1. 1 MarchInstalment 1: RM 500.00
  2. 1 MayInstalment 2: RM 500.00
  3. 1 JulyInstalment 3: RM 500.00
  4. 1 SeptemberInstalment 4: RM 500.00
  5. 1 NovemberInstalment 5: RM 500.00
  6. 1 JanuaryInstalment 6 (the next year): RM 500.00

How to pay an instalment

Pay online at ByrHASiL, by FPX from your bank account. There are 2 ways in:

  • Bayaran Menggunakan Nombor Bil (pay by bill number): LHDN suggests this one. No bill number? In MyTax, open Perkhidmatan ezHasil, then e-Billing, then Paparan Bil Pembayar Cukai.
  • Bayaran Menggunakan Nombor Pengenalan Cukai (TIN): choose the year and payment code 084, Bayaran Ansuran / Baki Cukai. ByrHASiL shows the bill for the instalment that is due.

You can also pay at Pos Malaysia, or at an appointed bank's counter, ATM or internet banking. The receipt shows in MyTax under Status MyTax > Lejar within 3 working days; if you see no payment status, do not pay a second time.

Revise the amount with CP502

If you expect this year's tax to differ from the CP500, ask LHDN to revise it with form CP502 (Borang Permohonan Pindaan Bayaran Ansuran).

  • When: once by 30 June, once by 31 October, or both. Later applications are not considered.
  • How: download CP502 from hasil.gov.my (Borang, then Muat Turun Borang, then Lain-Lain Borang). Send it to an LHDN office by hand or by post, or online through LHDN's Borang Maklum Balas Pelanggan.
  • What changes: only the amount, from your next instalment. The due dates and the number of instalments stay.

Suppose Daniel's work slows down, and in June he expects only RM 1,800.00 of tax for the year. He sends CP502 in June. March and May are already due, so the rest is spread over the 4 instalments left:

New total for the year
RM 1,800.00
Due in March and May
− RM 1,000.00
Left for July to January
RM 800.00

That makes each of the last 4 instalments RM 200.00.

Do not set it lower than the tax you expect. If you changed the amount and the year's tax (not counting any tax on a salary) is higher than your instalments by more than 30% of that tax, the part above 30% goes up by 10%.

Paying late

An instalment still unpaid 30 days after its due date goes up by 10% of the unpaid amount, with no further notice. LHDN may waive all or part of the increase if you show good cause.

For 2026, LHDN waived this increase for people with a salary as well as other income, such as rent, interest or royalties. LHDN told The Star in June 2026 that this does not cover people whose income is all from a business.

When you file Form B

CP500 is not extra tax: it comes off the year's tax when you file that year's Form B. In e-B, check the amount at Ansuran sendiri / CP500 (item B38).

Back to Daniel's RM 3,000.00 CP500, paid in full. Suppose his tax for the year is RM 3,400.00:

Daniel's tax for the year
RM 3,400.00
CP500 instalments paid
− RM 3,000.00
Still to pay with Form B
RM 400.00

Pay the rest by the Form B due date, 30 June (in 2026, LHDN gave e-B filers until 15 July). If your instalments were more than the tax, the difference comes back as a refund (Cukai terlebih bayar, item B39). The steps are in how to file Form B.

FAQ

I got no CP500. Do I still pay tax?

Yes. Without a CP500, you pay the whole year's tax when you file Form B; if you expected one, LHDN can issue it on request after February.

My income stopped this year. Can I stop paying CP500?

Not on your own: send CP502 with your new estimate by 30 June or 31 October. LHDN sends a new schedule if it agrees, or a letter (CP504) if it does not.

I only have a salary but got a CP500. What should I do?

LHDN's statement of 5 January 2026 says people with only a salary need not follow it. It advises them to update how their income is reported in their tax return, to avoid a CP500 in future.
