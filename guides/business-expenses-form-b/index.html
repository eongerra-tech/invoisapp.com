Which business expenses can you claim on Form B?

If you work for yourself in Malaysia, here is which business expenses you can deduct on Form B, which you cannot, how equipment is claimed and what to keep.

Invois team, EONGERRA TECH

8 min read · Last updated 3 November 2026

In short
  • The rule: an expense counts if you spent it wholly and exclusively to earn your business income.
  • You can claim materials, wages, rent, licences, utilities, transport to customers, ads and software. You cannot claim personal spending, what you pay yourself, fines or travel from home to your usual workplace.
  • Equipment: items of RM 2,000 or less each can be claimed in full the year you buy them, up to RM 20,000 a year. Items over RM 2,000: 20% plus the yearly rate in the first year, then 10% to 20% a year, by type.
  • Profit for Form B: income, minus expenses, minus capital allowances. Personal reliefs come after.

Step 2: List your expenses

Go through your receipts and bank statement, and sort each one with this table.

You can claim

  • Materials, parts, stock you resell
  • Wages for staff or helpers
  • Rent for a studio, workshop or shop
  • Business licence, utilities for the work space
  • Fuel, tolls, parking to customers and suppliers
  • Ads, website hosting, software subscriptions
  • Bank charges, interest on a business loan
  • Promotional gifts in Malaysia with your logo
  • Meals and entertainment for customersOnly half
  • An invoice you judge will never be paidIf counted as income in Step 1

You cannot claim

  • Personal and household spending
  • What you pay yourself, cash or stock you take
  • Travel from home to your usual workplace
  • Fines and penalties
  • Start-up costs before the business began
  • Equipment, vehicles, depreciationSee Step 3

Step 3: Take out the equipment

Equipment is not an expense. You claim it as capital allowance instead.

ItemHow it is claimed
RM 2,000 or less eachIn full the year you buy it, up to RM 20,000 a year. Not for items on hire purchase; those use the normal rates below.
More than RM 2,00020% plus the yearly rate in the first year, then the yearly rate until the cost is used up.
TypeYearly rateExamples
Motor vehicles, heavy machinery20%Van, motorcycle

Computers, software and cars have their own rules and limits, so ask an accountant which applies.

FAQ

Can I claim my laptop?

Yes, as capital allowance rather than an expense. At RM 2,000 or less it can be claimed in full the year you buy it. Above that, computers have their own rates, so ask an accountant which applies.

Can I claim an invoice the customer never paid?

Yes, if you counted it as income and, at the end of the year, you reasonably expect it will never be paid. Keep the reminders and letters that show you tried to collect.

Can I claim meals with customers?

Half of the cost, in most cases. Your own meals on a working day are personal and cannot be claimed.

Do I need an e-Invoice to claim an expense?

Not always. A supplier exempt from e-Invoice issues a normal receipt, and LHDN accepts it as proof of the expense.

What if my business made a loss?

In the same year, a business loss comes off your other income, such as a salary. What is left is carried forward against business income for up to 10 years in a row.
