Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Applies to the Invois mobile app and invoisapp.com

Invois is operated by Eongerra Tech, Malaysia. This policy describes what the app collects, who else processes it, where it is stored, and how you delete it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

  • We collect what is needed to produce your quotations and invoices — and your phone number, if you verify one.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Your voice notes and uploaded images are sent to OpenAI to be turned into text or extracted data. They are not used to train models.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app. Everything goes, permanently, after a 30-day grace period.

What we collect

All of it comes from you using the app. We do not buy data or collect it from other sources.

We do not collect your location, your contact list (only the single contact you pick in the system picker), your browsing, or an advertising ID.

These companies process data on our behalf, only to provide the service. None of them are permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Where it is stored

On servers in Singapore. Some processors above operate from other countries, so your data may be transferred outside Malaysia in the course of providing the service. It is encrypted in transit throughout.

How long we keep it

  • While your account exists — your documents stay until you delete them or your account.
  • Voice recordings are sent for transcription and not stored by us afterwards.
  • Verification codes expire within minutes.
  • After you delete your account — everything is kept for 30 days so you can change your mind, then permanently erased. See below.
  • Backups — routine backups are kept for up to 90 days, so a copy can outlive the erasure by up to that long. See below.

Deleting your account

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. Your account is disabled immediately and everything is permanently erased 30 days later — documents, customers, price list, images, your account record, and your data held by the processors above. Within those 30 days you can restore it and lose nothing.

If you have already uninstalled the app, use the account deletion page.

Backups are the one exception. We take routine backups of our database and file storage so that a fault on our side cannot lose your invoices. Those backups are kept for up to 90 days and then expire automatically, so a copy of your data can survive in a backup for up to 90 days after it is erased from the live system. Backups are encrypted at rest, are never used to serve the app, and are only ever restored to recover from an incident.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Invois Pro subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play and must be cancelled there, or Google will keep charging you.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you may ask for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. The app already gives you direct access to all of it — More → Export produces a copy of your documents. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 21 days.

Children

Invois is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
