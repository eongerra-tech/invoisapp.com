Describe the job out loud — “service two 1.5HP aircond, supply and install one new plug point” — and Invois turns it into an itemised quotation you can send to your customer in under a minute.

Built for people who work with their hands

Aircond technicians, tilers, electricians, plumbers — and equally photographers, tutors and hairdressers. If you quote jobs and chase payment, Invois is for you.

  • Voice first. Speak in Malay, English or Chinese — mixed is fine. Trade terms stay as trade terms: inverter, hacking, FOC, plug point.
  • Your prices, remembered. Build a price list once; it fills itself in after that.
  • Quotation → invoice → receipt. One tap between each. Nothing retyped.
  • Send on WhatsApp. A proper PDF, not a photo of a notebook page.

Malaysia first

SST, deposits and instalments, SSM and TIN fields on your letterhead, amounts in RM — set up the way work actually gets quoted here.
