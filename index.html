FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Email support@eongerra.com — a person reads it.

Is Invois free?

You can start free — no card needed. Documents made on the free plan carry a small “Made with invoisapp.com” line. Invois Pro is a subscription billed through Google Play; the price is shown in the app before you buy.

Which languages can I speak in?

Bahasa Melayu, English and Chinese — or a mix of all three in one sentence. Trade words like inverter, hacking, FOC and plug point stay as they are.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can use Invois straight away. Verifying your phone number by WhatsApp is optional, but it’s what lets you get your data back if you change or lose your phone.

How does my customer receive the quotation?

As a proper PDF you send on WhatsApp — or any app you like. When they agree, turn it into an invoice, then a receipt, with one tap each.

Does it handle SST, deposits and instalments?

Yes. Add SST, ask for a deposit, split the balance into instalments, and put your SSM and TIN numbers on your letterhead. Amounts are in RM.

Does Invois submit e-Invoices to LHDN?

No. Invois makes quotations, invoices and receipts for your customers. It doesn’t file anything with LHDN or any other authority on your behalf.

What happens to my voice recordings?

They’re sent to OpenAI to be turned into text, then not kept by us. They are not used to train AI models. Your data is stored on servers in Singapore. Details are in our privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Invois is on Android now; iOS is coming later.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. Everything is erased permanently after 30 days. Already uninstalled? Use the account deletion page. If you subscribed to Invois Pro, cancel it on Google Play too.
