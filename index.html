FAQ

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Email support@eongerra.com.

Is Invois free?

Yes, and no card is needed. Quotations, invoices, customers and your price list are unlimited. You get 15 AI actions a month, or 30 after you verify your phone. Free documents carry a small “Made with invoisapp.com” line. Invois Pro is RM 19.90 a month or RM 190 a year, with 300 AI actions a month and no footer line. It’s billed through Google Play, and you can cancel any time.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can use Invois straight away. Verifying your phone number by WhatsApp is optional, but it’s what lets you get your data back if you change or lose your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Invois is on Android now; iOS is coming soon.

Which languages does it support?

The whole app comes in Bahasa Melayu, English and Chinese. Pick one when you first open it, and switch any time under More → Language. You can also speak in any of the three, or mix them in one sentence. Words like inverter, FOC or pre-wedding stay as you say them.

Do I have to use voice?

No. You can build every quotation by picking items from your price list or typing a new one. Voice is there for when typing is slow, and it uses your AI allowance.

How does my customer receive the quotation?

As a PDF you send on WhatsApp or any other app. They open it like any PDF, without installing Invois.

Won’t an itemised quotation make my price look higher?

The total is the same. The customer can see what makes it up, so a big item like a gas top-up explains itself. If they want to spend less, they can drop an item, and the rest stays at your price.

Can I show that something is free?

Yes. Add it as an item at RM 0.00, for example travel or a first round of changes. It prints on the quotation at 0.00, so the customer sees what you include at no charge.

Does it handle SST, deposits and instalments?

Yes. Add SST, ask for a deposit, split the balance into instalments, and put your SSM and TIN numbers on your letterhead. Amounts are in RM.

Does Invois submit e-Invoices to LHDN?

No. Invois makes quotations, invoices and receipts for your customers. It doesn’t file anything with LHDN or any other authority on your behalf.

What happens to my voice recordings?

They’re sent to OpenAI to be turned into text. We don’t keep them, and they aren’t used to train AI models. Your data is stored on servers in Singapore. Details are in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. Everything is erased permanently after 30 days. Already uninstalled? Use the account deletion page. If you subscribed to Invois Pro, cancel it on Google Play too.
